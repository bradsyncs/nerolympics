THE COMPETITION

More than just a game.

The Nerolympics brings the Nerotopia community together through a week of competition, teamwork, and unforgettable events.

Teams will be randomly assigned to help keep the competition fair. Participants will compete in different events throughout the week, earning opportunities to win exclusive rewards. Hosted by Nero and the Staff Team.
